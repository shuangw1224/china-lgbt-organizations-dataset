# Mapping LGBT+ Organizations in China

**An Original Dataset | Ongoing Research Project**

## Overview

This project constructs an original, multi-source database of LGBT+ organizations in mainland China to examine how social movements emerge and develop under political constraints and transnational influences.

Drawing on NGO directories, archived webpages, historical mailing-list archives, and publicly available social media sources, the project seeks to identify formal and informal organizations that are often absent from conventional NGO registries.

The database is designed to support longitudinal analysis of organizational development, collective identity framing, and advocacy strategies within China's LGBT+ movement.

## Research Objectives

The project seeks to document the organizational landscape of China's LGBT+ movement and examine variation in organizational trajectories, identity categories, and repertoires of collective action.

## Current Progress

**Status: Data construction and validation in progress**

- Identified over 200 organizations through multi-source archival research.
- Developed a preliminary workflow for candidate extraction, name normalization, deduplication, and source verification.
- Continuing manual review of organizational eligibility and historical identities.
- Developing an organization-level codebook for subsequent longitudinal analysis.
