# Mapping LGBT+ Organizations in China

**An Original Dataset | Research in Progress**

**Researcher:** Shuang Wu  
**Documentation updated:** November 12, 2026

## Overview

This project builds an organization-level database of LGBT+ organizations in mainland China, documenting formal NGOs, informal community groups, student organizations, and HIV/AIDS-related groups with documented connections to sexual and gender minority communities.

The database is being developed to support research on organizational development, collective identity framing, and advocacy strategies under political constraints and transnational influences. The current work focuses on identifying organizations and assessing their supporting evidence; longitudinal coding and analysis are planned for later stages.

## Current Progress

The initial source is the **China AIDS Group mailing-list archive**. More than 7,000 email bodies have been processed through an AI-assisted extraction and screening workflow. Attachments have not yet been systematically processed.

The Stage 2 cleaning report records:

- **9,012 candidate names** after initial name consolidation.
- **208 provisional mainland LGBTQ-related candidate records** selected for the working master, including **167 not in the Stage 1 seed list**.
- **1,789 candidates** awaiting further verification.

Online corroboration and researcher review are ongoing.

See [China AIDS Group: Stage 2 Cleaning Report](china_aids_cleaning_report.md) for the workflow, counts, and remaining work.

## Research Approach

The researcher collects sources, defines the research scope, and reviews candidate records. AI agents assist with extracting names and evidence, consolidating name variants, screening candidates, and locating corroborating webpages. Technical infrastructure is maintained by [@lfkdsk](https://github.com/lfkdsk) through Techne-Nexus.

Review distinguishes three questions: whether an identifiable organization exists, whether different references concern the same entity, and whether the entity meets the project's inclusion criteria. HIV/AIDS-related activity alone does not establish LGBT+ relevance.

See [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) for further details on sources, evidence, review procedures, and limitations.

## Next Steps

- Resolve ambiguous names, possible duplicates, and relationships between projects, branches, and parent organizations.
- Extend source coverage to email attachments, archived webpages, Weibo materials, and online forums.
- Develop an organization-level codebook for historical trajectories, identity categories, and activities.

## Data Availability

This repository presents documentation and preliminary progress from an ongoing research project. Raw correspondence and personal information are not included in these public reports. Any release of organization-level data will depend on further validation and assessment of source-access conditions and disclosure risks.
